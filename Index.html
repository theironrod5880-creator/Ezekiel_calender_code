Counts, as written in the text (Tier 2): four living creatures · four faces each · four wings each · four wheels · one wheel within a wheel per wheel · rims full of eyes · one expanse · one seat · one likeness of adam upon it · one measuring reed · one cord of flax · one altar of four squared stages · one river · trees on both banks · one city of twelve gates.

Two phrase-fits carried forward: ◆ regel yesharah (1:7) and nechoshet qalal (1:7) are both drawn from a single verse and both appear as separate features in the project's existing code. Recorded as correspondence only.

Not in this index: Ezekiel's use of merkabah — the word does not occur anywhere in the book. The chariot is described, never named. The later title for the vision is outside the text.

Excluded by design: family material, personal names, dates, day-counts, and all organization naming. This index is seal-clean.
